# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN | deprecated
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: the maintainer

## Context

What situation forces a decision, which constraints apply (cost, time,
skills, product requirements) and which facts were verified, with their
source and date.

## Decision

What is decided, in one or two paragraphs, with the concrete mechanism.

## Alternatives considered

For each alternative: what it would give, what it would cost, and the
specific reason it was not chosen. An alternative that was never a real
option does not belong here.

## Consequences

- Positive: what becomes easier or safer.
- Negative: what becomes harder, slower or more expensive, and what is
  accepted knowingly.
- The strongest argument against this decision, stated fairly.

## Reopen when

Observable conditions under which this decision must be revisited.

## Links

Related ADRs, sections of PLAN.md, external sources with consultation date.
