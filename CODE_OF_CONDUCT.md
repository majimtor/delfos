# Code of conduct

Delfos is a one-person portfolio project; its issues and pull requests are
a workplace all the same.

- Discuss the code, the decisions and the evidence, never the person.
- Assume good faith; disagree with arguments, and accept the maintainer's
  decision once it is explained.
- No harassment, no discriminatory remarks, and no personal data of anyone
  in issues, pull requests or commits.
- The maintainer may edit, hide or close any contribution that breaks these
  rules, and may block whoever repeats it.

A concern about conduct is raised in the thread where it happened or, if
that is not possible, in a new issue addressed to the maintainer, who
answers within seven days.
